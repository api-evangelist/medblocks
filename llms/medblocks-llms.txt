# Medblocks
> Medblocks provides Health IT development services, training bootcamps, and open-source
tools for building healthcare applications using FHIR, openEHR, and SNOMED CT standards.
Founded by Sidharth Ramesh.
## Company Overview
- [Homepage](https://www.medblocks.com): Health IT services company helping organizations
build standard-based healthcare software
- [About / Story](https://www.medblocks.com/blog/the-story-of-medblocks): Origin story of
Medblocks, from blockchain prototype to healthcare interoperability
## Services
- [Sprint - Development as a Service](https://www.medblocks.com/sprint): Rapid Health IT
development using FHIR and openEHR standards, advocating for software ownership over SaaS
subscriptions
## Training
- [FHIR Bootcamp](https://www.medblocks.com/training/courses/fhir-bootcamp): Build 5 FHIR
applications in 10 weeks (300+ students enrolled)
- [openEHR Bootcamp](https://www.medblocks.com/training/courses/openehr-bootcamp): Build 5
openEHR applications in 10 weeks (250+ students)
- [FHIR Fundamentals](https://www.medblocks.com/training/courses/fhir-fundamentals): Free
introductory FHIR course (500+ students)
- [openEHR Fundamentals](https://www.medblocks.com/training/courses/openehr-fundamentals):
Introductory openEHR course (50+ students)
